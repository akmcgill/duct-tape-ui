Section 01 — what gets measured

Eight dimensions, weighted into one number.

Every organization has duct tape somewhere. The diagnostic finds the seams that are actually load-bearing — and won't let a strong average hide a critical one.

D01 · CORE

Workflow Maturity

How much of the work is documented process versus remembered process.

D02 · CORE

Legacy Knowledge Risk

Single-point-of-failure check. Fires a knockout at the lowest anchor.

D03 · CORE

Operational Clarity

Whether the SOP survives contact with an exception. Branching-SOP knockout.

D04 · HUMAN

Human Adoption Readiness

Curiosity or workaround. Active-sabotage knockout at the lowest anchor.

D05 · HUMAN

Leadership Alignment

Whether the people funding the work agree on what the work is.

D06 · HUMAN

Organizational Learning Capacity

How much change the organization can absorb before fatigue sets in.

D07 · GOV

Governance & Trust

Who signs off, who gets told, and what actually gets written down.

D08 · GOV

Data Reliability

Whether the system number matches the number people use. Shadow-source knockout.

!
Four dimensions can override the whole score.

A single catastrophic answer can't be averaged away. If one of the four knockout conditions fires, the composite is forced to red and the trigger is named in your report.

Section 02 — how it runs

A conversation, then a readout.

Step 01

Eight questions

Plain language, adapted to your role. An ops leader and an L&D lead don't get the same wording.

Step 02

Deterministic scoring

Weighted pillar averages, per-dimension banding, knockout override. Same answers, same score, every time.

Step 03

Your readout

Composite score, eight-dimension heatmap, critical flags, and a prioritized read on what to shore up first.
